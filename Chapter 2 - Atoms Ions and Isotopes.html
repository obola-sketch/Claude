Atoms, ions and isotopes, one particle at a time

Three counts decide everything on this page. Protons fix which element you have and can never change without changing the element. Neutrons change only the mass, giving you isotopes. Electrons change only the charge, giving you ions. Move each one and watch which parts of the symbol move with it.
